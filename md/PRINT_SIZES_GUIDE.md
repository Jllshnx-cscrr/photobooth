## 🎨 DPI Explained

### What is DPI?
**DPI** (Dots Per Inch) = How many pixels fit in one inch when printed.

### DPI Settings

| DPI | Quality | Best For | File Size |
|-----|---------|----------|-----------|
| **150** | Screen/Web | Digital display, social media | Small |
| **300** | Standard Print | Most photo prints, photo booths | Medium |
| **600** | High Quality | Professional prints, large formats | Large |

### When to Use Each DPI

**150 DPI:**
- Viewing on screens only
- Social media sharing
- Email attachments
- Quick preview prints

**300 DPI (Recommended):**
- ✅ Standard photo booth prints
- ✅ 4×6" to 8×10" photos
- ✅ Most commercial printing
- ✅ Good quality at reasonable file size

**600 DPI:**
- Professional photography
- Large format prints (>8×10")
- Archive quality
- When print quality is critical

## 🎯 Quick Calculator

### Formula: Pixels = Inches × DPI

**Examples:**
- 4" × 300 DPI = 1200 pixels
- 6" × 300 DPI = 1800 pixels
- 2" × 300 DPI = 600 pixels
- 8" × 300 DPI = 2400 pixels

### Reverse Formula: Inches = Pixels ÷ DPI

**Examples:**
- 1200 px ÷ 300 DPI = 4 inches
- 1800 px ÷ 300 DPI = 6 inches
- 2400 px ÷ 300 DPI = 8 inches

## 💡 Template Designer Tips

### In the App:
1. Click the **"Inches"** button in template settings
2. Choose your **DPI** (300 recommended)
3. Enter dimensions in inches (much easier!)
4. Pixel values are automatically calculated

### Example: Creating a 2×6" Strip at 300 DPI
1. Toggle to **Inches**
2. Set DPI to **300**
3. Enter Width: **2.0**
4. Enter Height: **6.0**
5. Result: 600 × 1800 pixels automatically!

## 🎨 Design Considerations

### For 2×6" Strips:
- Design 4 photo slots vertically
- Each slot approximately 2" × 1.25"
- Leave margins for borders/text

### For 4×6" Photos:
- 2×2 grid (4 slots)
- Or 1 large + 3 small slots
- Portrait or landscape orientation

### For Square Formats:
- 2×2 grid works perfectly
- Equal spacing looks best
- Consider circular slots

## 🔧 In the Template Designer

### Setting Template Size:

**Pixels Mode:**
- Enter exact pixel dimensions
- Good if you know your printer specs
- Example: 1200 × 1800

**Inches Mode (Easier!):**
- Enter physical print size
- Choose DPI from dropdown
- Pixels calculated automatically
- Example: 4" × 6" @ 300 DPI

### Setting Slot Size:

The same toggle works for photo slots:
- **Pixels:** Exact pixel positioning
- **Inches:** Physical size on print

Both modes show the alternate measurement in parentheses!

## 📏 Measurement Tips

### Think in Physical Size
